# International Diploma in Geodesy, GNSS and SIRGAS

During the month of February, our technical team took part in the **International Diploma in Geodesy, GNSS and SIRGAS**, a high-level training program that reinforces our commitment to professional excellence.

## Why is it important?

Geodesy is the science that studies the shape, dimensions and gravitational field of the Earth. In the context of modern surveying, geodetic knowledge is essential for:

- **Centimetric precision** in large-scale surveys
- **Coordinate transformation** between different reference systems
- **Integration with national networks** such as SIRGAS (Geocentric Reference System for the Americas)

## Diploma Content

The program included modules on:

1. **Theory of the Terrestrial Reference System**
2. **GNSS observations and data processing**
3. **Active and passive geodetic networks**
4. **Coordinate transformation between systems**
5. **Practical applications in cadastral surveys**

## Benefits for our clients

This training allows us to offer:

- Greater precision in surveys of large areas
- Better integration with geographic information systems
- Compliance with international standards in geodesy
- Optimization of data processing times
